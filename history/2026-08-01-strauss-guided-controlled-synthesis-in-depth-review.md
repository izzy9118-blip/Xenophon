## Review basis

The synthesis was reviewed against the complete printed order of Leo Strauss's "Xenophon's Anabasis," book pages 105-136, the fifty-one-unit primary reconstruction, the owner-adopted R3 comparison, and the authorial-revelation correction `XEN-COR-003`.

## Result

The synthesis was not adopted. It was returned for additive targeted R1 revision.

The review confirmed that the draft successfully preserves Xenophon as revealing author, distinguishes the narrated Xenophon, retains the eight-movement sequence, preserves all nineteen unresolved questions, keeps material governance and Thibron independent, and restores the exact ending of Socratic apprehension, booty, gods, and oaths.

Three blocking defects remain.

First, the central thesis and Movement VI blur cross-work jurisdiction. The older Cyrus is Xenophon's presentation in the *Education of Cyrus*, not a figure presented within the *Anabasis*. Strauss brings that Xenophontic presentation into his interpretation of the *Anabasis*.

Second, the synthesis converts Strauss's questions into assertions. Strauss asks whether knowledge of rule needs an iron alloy, whether prescription is indispensable to legitimacy and non-tyrannical government, and what justice means. The synthesis may not present those questions as a completed political model.

Third, the revised synthesis must restore the decisive direction of the passage: Xenophon, by standing between the older Cyrus and Socrates, presents to the reader the problem of justice. The older Cyrus is material within Xenophon's revelation of that problem, not an external standard acting upon him.
