The final unit covers IV.8 on one-based PDF pages 90-93. At the Macrone border, an unnamed light infantryman who reports prior enslavement at Athens recognizes the local speech and mediates a parley. The Macrones identify the Greeks as invaders. The generals deny harmful intent, explain their war with the king and return toward Hellas, and exchange a Macrone and Greek lance as reciprocal pledges under divine witness. The Macrones then cut trees, build the crossing road, mingle with the army, provide a market, and guide the Greeks for three days.

At the Colchian mountain, Xenophon rejects a continuous line on broken ground and proposes company columns with intervals, mutual support, and flank extension. After prayer and battle hymn, enemy redeployment opens the center. Aeschines's Arcadian light infantry mistakes the movement for flight and rushes the summit, followed by Cleanor's hoplites, and the Colchians flee.

In the captured villages, local honey disables hundreds through vomiting, diarrhea, drunkenness-like states, and madness-like collapse. No deaths are reported, and recovery follows over the next several days. The botanical explanation in the witness note remains editorial paratext rather than primary narrative evidence.

The army reaches Trapezus, halts about thirty days, receives market and hospitality from the city, ravages surrounding Colchian territory, fulfills vows to Zeus the Saviour, Heracles, and other gods, and holds games under Dracontius. Captive boys compete in the short race, more than sixty Cretans run the long race, and the festival includes wrestling, boxing, pankration, and a steep horse race.

The Book V retrospective synopsis begins after IV.8 and remains classified as witness paratext. The next production unit is `XEN-PRI-RU-030`, covering *Anabasis* V.1 on PDF pages 94-95.
