## Governing substantive findings

- Strauss's essay must be reconstructed sequentially, not only topically.
- Proxenos must be restored as the mediator who brings Xenophon from Cyrus, wealth, power, and uprootedness toward the Socratic comparison.
- The economic art must connect material governance with Xenophon's difference from Socrates.
- Piety must include toughness, wit, wiliness, `deus sive casus`, divine omniscience, and the unanswered question of what a god is.
- Justice must include coercion, trial, acquittal, fatherland, legitimacy, degrees of cruelty, the mutual dependence of manly and Socratic virtue, and Xenophon as the closest approximation to their coexistence.
- Founding, monarchy, ambition, and fatherland must be reconstructed as successive tests of Xenophon's ascent.
- Xenophon's acquittal and apologies must be read against Socrates' trial and apology.
- The ending must preserve Seuthes, unpaid wages, accusation, Zeus the King, reconciliation, Greece and Athens, booty, Thibron, gods, oaths, and Socrates' final question.
- Strauss guides the architecture, but the independent primary reconstruction may still disclose distributed evidence and institutional patterns that Strauss did not separately formulate.
