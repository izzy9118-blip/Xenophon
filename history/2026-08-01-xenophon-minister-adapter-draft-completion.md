## Pedagogical duty

The Xenophon adapter orders the path from a concrete deed or material condition, through the governing Socrates–Cyrus alternative, through Xenophon's authorial arrangement, to the narrowest supported judgment. The judgment is stated directly; pedagogy may order the difficulty but may not hide the answer.
