# Xenophontic Authorial-Revelation Direction Correction

Date: 2026-07-31
Status: OWNER ADOPTED
Correction: XEN-COR-003

The owner corrected a reversal in the interpretive direction of the adopted R3 comparison. The older Cyrus does not supply Xenophon with an external standard. Xenophon supplies the presentation of the older Cyrus to the reader. Through this presentation, Xenophon reveals—indirectly and with deliberate concealment—his understanding of rule, inheritance, prescription, cruelty, manly justice, and political viability.

Strauss's function is correspondingly clarified. Strauss does not import a standard into Xenophon. He uncovers the concealed order of Xenophon's differentiated presentations: the younger Cyrus, the older Cyrus, Socrates, and Xenophon's narrated self-presentation.

The correction preserves the distinction and intended ambiguity between the two Cyruses while placing authorial agency where it belongs. Xenophon is the revealing author. His manner of revelation—composition, sequence, ambiguity, omission, contrast, narration, and concealment—is itself part of the evidence.
