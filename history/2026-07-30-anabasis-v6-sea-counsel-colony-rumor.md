## Documentary advancement

The unit records the public consultation with the Sinopeans, Hecatonymus's argument that the Paphlagonian land route is impossible, the vote for sea travel under an all-or-none shipping condition, Xenophon's unannounced colony thought and sacrifice, Silanus's rumor, the intervention of Timasion and Thorax through merchants and privately encouraged payment promises, the public accusation and defense, the anti-desertion vote, the withholding of promised pay, and the resulting proposal to seize Phasian territory.

## Preserved tensions

- Public counsel and voting coexist with suspected bias, private gratuities, rumor networks, and officer canvassing.
- The army refuses divided embarkation in the name of collective safety while threatening an individual who claims a right to depart.
- Xenophon renounces the colony project while stating that superior force exercises a right over the property of the weaker.
- The leaders who argue for return to Hellas pivot toward territorial seizure when promised pay fails.
