# Controlled Comparison Correction — Strauss as Guiding Architecture

Date: 2026-07-31

## Owner finding

The first controlled-comparison draft drifted by treating Leo Strauss's reconstruction as one parallel interpretive stream at the very points where the Socrates-Cyrus opposition should govern the examination.

Three errors were identified:

1. Material governance across all seven books and Thibron's incorporation of the army were classified as `SECONDARY_SILENCE`. They belong on the Cyrus side: economic art, acquisition, command, pay, booty, institutional rule, renewed war, and service.
2. Justice was classified as `DIVERGENCE`. The concrete disputes over promises, punishment, pay, booty, testimony, distribution, and coercion are the evidentiary heart of Strauss's examination of manly and Socratic justice.
3. The ending was divided into two equal closures. Strauss is the guiding light: Thibron, renewed Persian war, booty, incomplete homecoming, fatherland, piety, and Socrates' apprehension belong to one final Socrates-Cyrus examination.

## Governing rule

Strauss supplies the guiding interpretive architecture; Xenophon's primary reconstruction supplies the speeches, deeds, institutions, material conditions, and outcomes that must be examined. The evidence stream remains distinct, but the comparison may not manufacture false symmetry by treating Strauss's Socrates-Cyrus argument as merely one optional closure.

The correction restores the examination; it does not complete it. Greek wording, Themistogenes, numerical structures, divine causation, cross-work claims, and the final relation between manly and Socratic virtue remain unresolved.
