# Hieron / On Tyranny Completed-Work Integration

On 7 August 2026 the owner directed the repository to integrate the already completed *On Tyranny* witness audit and close-reading work without restarting it or representing it as unaudited.

The integration registers Xenophon's *Hiero or Tyrannicus* as the primary work under investigation; Strauss's *On Tyranny* as the guiding interpretive witness; Kojeve's *Tyranny and Wisdom* as the independent testing challenge; Strauss's *Restatement on Xenophon's Hiero* as a distinct later response; the Strauss-Kojeve correspondence as documentary inquiry history; and the Gourevitch-Roth editorial apparatus as documentary control.

The completed predecessor production includes the *Hieron*, Strauss, Kojeve, and Restatement reconstructions; forty-four correspondence units through Strauss's final surviving letter of 3 June 1965; an editorial and material-witness audit; a correction preserving the editorially attested but normally unavailable Kojeve-to-Strauss letter of 5 November 1957; and Integrated Synthesis Unit 48, “The Whole of *On Tyranny*.”

The owner-supplied searchable 2013 composite witness is not deposited in this repository. Its prior availability and completed use are preserved, but its filename and SHA-256 were not present in the current handoff. The repository therefore records those fields as unavailable rather than inventing them or copying copyrighted source text.

This production unit is additive. It does not revise any *Anabasis* record, alter the active English-witness jurisdiction of the authorized adapter, claim Greek philology, confer owner adoption, modify Sanctum registration, or authorize Assembly dispatch.

The normalized package is complete as an integration draft and awaits bounded in-depth owner review. That review is to verify admission and repository normalization, not to repeat the completed close reading from the beginning.
